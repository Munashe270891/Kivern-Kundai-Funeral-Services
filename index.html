Policy Terms & Conditions

Important operational terms and claims information

Cover commences after a 90-day waiting period from the commencement date for member, spouse, or own children aged 25 years and below. Accidental death cover is immediate subject to policy rules. Individual plans carry a 2 to 3 month waiting period; group plans carry no waiting period.

Children are covered up to the age of 25 years provided they are unmarried. Covered lives must carry the same surname as the proposer unless registered as extended family.

A 15-day grace period is allowed for late monthly premium payments. In the event of non-payment beyond grace periods, the policy will lapse. Payments are done at Kivern & Kundai offices, and valid receipts must be produced.

Upon a death occurrence, inform Kivern & Kundai immediately. Documents required include: Notice of Death or Burial Order, authentic ID documentation of deceased, national identity card or birth certificate for children.

No services or benefits will be rendered to persons who commit suicide within 2 years from the date of policy commencement or renewal.
