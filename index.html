Policy Terms & Conditions

[cite: 4, 5]

Important operational terms and claims information

1. Waiting Periods & Commencement

Cover commences after a 90-day waiting period from the commencement date for member, spouse, or own children aged 25 years and below. Accidental death cover is immediate subject to policy rules[cite: 4]. Individual plans carry a 2 to 3 month waiting period; group plans carry no waiting period[cite: 5].

2. Age & Surname Guidelines

Children are covered up to the age of 25 years provided they are unmarried[cite: 2, 4]. Covered lives must carry the same surname as the proposer unless registered as extended family[cite: 2].

3. Premium Payments & Grace Periods

A 15-day grace period is allowed for late monthly premium payments[cite: 4]. In the event of non-payment beyond grace periods, the policy will lapse[cite: 2, 4]. Payments are done at Kivern & Kundai offices, and valid receipts must be produced[cite: 2].

4. Required Claims Documentation

Upon a death occurrence, inform Kivern & Kundai immediately[cite: 2, 4]. Documents required include: Notice of Death or Burial Order, authentic ID documentation of deceased, national identity card or birth certificate for children[cite: 4].

5. General Exclusions

No services or benefits will be rendered to persons who commit suicide within 2 years from the date of policy commencement or renewal[cite: 2, 4].
